AdoptMe Values — Market Research & Strategy Validation

Date: 2026-06-19 · Independent deep research to verify and improve the marketing strategy. Sources and confidence levels included.

Method note: Discord/Reddit/Facebook pages are login- or JS-gated, so some counts come from search snippets quoting live listings and are labelled accordingly. App Store / Google Play and Similarweb pages rendered fully. Nothing is fabricated; uncertain figures are flagged.

Executive summary — what the research changed

The existing strategy is directionally correct and well-built. Research confirmed its core bets (Discord first, 90/10 value ratio, warm aged accounts, own-Discord-server, share-card idea, ASO priority). It also surfaced nine corrections/additions to make before handing the plan to a hire:

  1. “Pet Folio” is your own app, not a competitor. App Store id6745400111 has also been branded “Pet Folio,” and its developer site is adoptmevalues.app — your domain. Remove it from the competitor list. (Confirmed by you: it’s yours.)
  2. The “rivals are mostly websites; we have an app” wedge is half-right. True for amvgg.com, adoptmevalues.gg, adoptmetradingvalues.com (web-only). False for elvebredd.com and Traderie — both ship iOS + Android apps. The sharper wedge is a polished, SAFE, Adopt-Me-focused app with live chat + alerts.
  3. Your Discord target list is missing the biggest servers. “Adopt Me Trade” (~275k) is larger than AMTC (~238k); cross-trading hubs (~668k) exist. AMTC/AMTV counts are otherwise accurate.
  4. Don’t overweight adoptmevalues.gg — small (~223k visits/mo) and falling fast (-25% MoM). Real leaders: elvebredd (~13.2M) and amvgg (~4.4M).
  5. The §8 referral design violates app-store policy as written. “Invite a friend → both unlock a reward” rewards the invitee for downloading, which Apple and Google prohibit. Fix: reward the REFERRER only, non-cash, never tied to reviews.
  6. TikTok now requires the Commercial Content disclosure toggle on promo videos (Sept 13 2025) — undisclosed promo is ineligible for the For You feed.
  7. YouTube Shorts links are NON-clickable in descriptions and comments. Route via the channel-profile link + a Short → long-form video path.
  8. Audience is teens (13+), not under-13 kids — now a hard compliance line. Discord teen-by-default (March 2026), Reddit age-assurance, YouTube “Made for Kids” stripping, and COPPA all apply. Never DM minors; have counsel check COPPA posture.
  9. ASO “do it first” is strongly validated — ratings/sentiment is the single biggest lever. Competitors (incl. your own app) sit ~4.4★ and bleed stars over ad load, value accuracy, and chat scam/safety. That’s both your opening and your risk.

3. Organic growth — 2025–2026 algorithm realities

The cross-platform reality to add: audience = TEENS (13+), not kids

  • TikTok (Sept 13 2025): undisclosed promo is For-You-ineligible — use the built-in Commercial Content disclosure toggle.
  • Discord: teen-by-default settings rolling out globally from early March 2026; don’t depend on DMing teens or Stage events.
  • YouTube “Made for Kids”: disables comments, end screens, Community posts, links — aim content at general/teen audience.

TikTok (highest discovery ROI — confirmed)

  • Follower count is not a ranking factor; cold-start test batch surfaces new accounts. Niche consistency in the first 5–10 videos helps.
  • Post 3–5×/week; 3–5 spaced/day is safe; 10+ rapid/near-duplicate posts risks shadowban.
  • Hook in first 2–3 seconds; captions mandatory (~85% watch muted); TikTok is a search engine — keywords in caption + on-screen text + audio.
  • Links: none clickable in captions/comments; clickable bio link needs 1,000 followers on personal accounts (Business: none). Use a link-in-bio landing page.

Reddit (confirmed; strict)

  • Warm new accounts 1–2 weeks (comment only). #1 ban trigger: same link across 5+ subs. Never >2 link-shares/day; no shorteners (use full UTM links).
  • 9:1 self-promo rule is de-facto enforced. Native posts + link in a comment beat link posts. Enforcement ~96% automated.

YouTube Shorts (correct the link mechanics)

  • Subscriber count largely irrelevant; first 1–3s + “Viewed vs Swiped Away” decide reach; ~200+ Shorts builds momentum.
  • Links in Shorts descriptions/comments are NON-clickable. Use channel-profile links + Short → long-form → clickable link.
  • Recycled/near-duplicate content is now actively penalized (Jul 15 2025 update).

Discord growth (confirmed, with gates)

  • No discovery feed. Server Discovery needs ≥1,000 members, ≥8 weeks, Community server. Partner program paused; Quests are paid. Until then: directory listings + external traffic.
  • Your companion bot cannot be a marketing channel — Developer Policy bans using your app/bot to advertise or mass-DM users.

4. ASO — confirmed as the #1 free lever

  1. Ratings & reviews — biggest conversion + ranking lever. 3★→4★ can lift install conversion ~89%; sub-3.5★ apps lose keyword visibility. Use native review APIs (iOS SKStoreReviewController ≤3/yr; Google Play In-App Review) fired after a successful WFL check. Never incentivize or gate. Reply to reviews.
  2. Icon + first 2–3 screenshots drive the download decision (~7-sec scan). A/B test; build screenshots around the “WIN / FAIR / LOSE” moment.
  3. Keywords (platform-specific): iOS = Title + Subtitle + hidden 100-char keyword field (no spaces after commas, no repeats, no brand). Google = keyword-rich Title + Short Description (80, weighted) + Long Description (indexed, ~2–3% density).

Also: install velocity + retention now jointly drive ranking (build daily-return reasons). Localize early. New iOS levers: Custom Product Pages appear in organic search, In-App Events, LLM review summaries (18.4+). Add an “unofficial, not affiliated” disclaimer (every competitor does). Verdict: fully confirms §8’s “ASO first.”

5. Ethical install tactics — validated, with one policy fix

  • Referral loops: double-sided non-cash rewards tied to product value work (Dropbox, Duolingo); healthy k-factor ≈ 0.3–0.7. But: reward the REFERRER only — never the invitee for downloading, never tie rewards to reviews (Apple 3.2.2/5.6.3; Google policy).
  • Share mechanics (strongest fit): spoiler-free, status result cards that deep-link back (Wordle, Spotify Wrapped, Strava). For you: a “Win/Fair/Lose” / “my inventory is worth X” card, pre-sized for TikTok/Shorts/Discord, deep-linked to the app.
  • Ambassador/creator programs: creator codes that reward the creator’s audience with non-cash perks (Brawl Stars; Epic Support-A-Creator 5%/$100). Avoid cash bounties to minors. This is the clean version of your “item 7.”
  • Owned Discord: exclusivity + giveaways with bonus entries for inviting friends = lowest-cost, highest-fit loop.
Kid-audience caveats: COPPA likely applies (parental consent, careful data use); the FTC counts “incentives” toward “directed to children.” Use non-cash in-app rewards only, run giveaways with explicit age/parental rules, and have counsel confirm COPPA posture. (Not legal advice.)
