# AdoptMe Values — Marketing Plan (Hand-Over Version)

**For:** the marketing person who will do the daily work.
**Read this whole file once before you start. Then keep it open and follow it every day.**

**The job in one sentence:** Go to the places where Adopt Me traders *already are* (Discord, Reddit, Facebook), help them for free, and gently bring them to our app and website.

**No videos.** This plan is **text + image posts only** — no filming, no editing. Everything you post is either a message, a written post, or a screenshot from the app.

**What we are promoting (already built and live):**
- **Website:** https://www.adoptmevalues.app/
- **iOS app:** https://apps.apple.com/lv/app/adoptme-values-calculator/id6745400111
- **Android app:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adoptmevaluescalc

**The goal:** more **real** installs and **real** website visits. Real people only. **No bots, no fake accounts, no fake reviews, no paying for installs.** Fake activity can get our app deleted from the store. This is the most important rule in the whole plan.

---

## 1. The ONE rule that protects everything

> **Help people first. Promote second.**

For every **10 things you post: 9 help, 1 promotes** (the **90/10 rule**).

If you only drop links, you get banned and the work is wasted. If you help people, they trust you — and *then* your one promo works. A trusted helper's link survives; a stranger's link gets deleted.

**The 10% is a ceiling, not a quota.** Only promote when it genuinely helps the person in front of you. If a day gives no natural moment, post **zero** promos — never force a link in just to "hit the number."

> **🚨 Escalation rule.** If you are unsure about any of these — posting a link, mentioning the app, or whether a server/subreddit's rules allow something — **STOP and ask the manager before you post.** When in doubt, don't.

## 2. The strategy (why we do it this way)

There are two ways to get an audience:
1. **Go to where people already are** (big Discord servers, subreddits, Facebook groups). — **This is our main job.** Fast, free, thousands of people already there.
2. **Build our own audience from zero** (our own Discord server). — Slow. We do this *second*, as a "home base" to keep the people we meet.

**You spend ~90% of your time on #1.** You go to them. You do not wait for them to come to us.

**The funnel (how a stranger becomes our user):**

> They ask a trade question in a Discord/Reddit/Facebook group → **you give a genuinely helpful answer** → over days they see you help again and again → they trust you → you softly mention the app *when it actually helps* → they install it → some of them join our own Discord → they refer their trading friends.

Every day, your work is just feeding this funnel.

## 3. Channels — in priority order (all post-based, no video)

| # | Channel | What you do there | Effort | Speed of results |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Discord** (big trading servers) | Help in WFL / "is this a win?" channels. Post value updates where allowed. | Medium | **Fast** |
| **2** | **Reddit** (trading subs) | Comment-help + occasional image/text posts (value roundups, screenshots). | Low–Med | Medium |

**Start with Discord + Reddit (weeks 1–4). Add Facebook in week 3. TikTok Photo Mode is optional** — only if you have spare time after the first three are running well. Skip YouTube (it needs video).

> **Why TikTok Photo Mode is allowed here:** TikTok lets you post a swipeable set of *images* with a caption and a sound — no filming or editing. It still gets real reach. This is the only "video platform" we touch, and we only use its photo feature.

## 4. Your accounts — keep it small and real

We do **NOT** run many fake accounts. We run a few **real, trusted** identities. One trusted account beats ten new ones.

| Platform | How many accounts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Discord** | **1 account only** | Many accounts = instant ban. One real trader persona. |
| **Reddit** | **1 account only** | Reddit detects multi-accounts instantly. |
| **Facebook** | **1 personal** (+ a Page if you want) | — |
| **TikTok** (optional) | **1 Business account** | Business account = link allowed in bio. |

**THE HARD RULE — never break this:**

> Your accounts must **NEVER** like, follow, upvote, or reply to each other to look popular. No account asking "is this app good?" and another answering "yes I love it!". That is fake engagement. Platforms see the accounts are linked (same phone/internet) and ban them — and the app can be removed from the store.

**Setting up each account:**
1. Use a **separate Gmail** for each. Keep a list (email = which account) and share it with the manager.
2. Add a **real, friendly photo and a full bio** to each. Empty profiles look like bots.
3. If it asks for a **phone number, use a real one.** Never buy fake numbers.
4. **Warm each account for 7–14 days before any promotion** (see section 5).

## 5. Week 1–2 — Setup and warm-up (NO links yet)

New accounts that post links too early get blocked. First, you look like a normal, helpful trader.

**Week 1 — Setup**
- Day 1: Read this whole plan. Install both apps + open the website. Practice until you can do a WFL check, read the value list, and set an alert in under 1 minute.
- Day 2: Create the accounts (space them out, don't make all at once). Fill every profile fully.
- Day 3: Join the Discord servers, subreddits, and Facebook groups from the **Target List (section 9)**. **Read each one's rules.** For each place write down: *"Promo allowed? Yes / No / Only in #self-promo channel."*
- Day 4: Ask the manager for the **tracked links** (section 8).
- Day 5: Using the scripts in section 7, write yourself **20 helpful reply templates** and **15 post ideas** so daily work is fast.

**Week 2 — Warm up (still no links / no app mentions)**
- ~30 min per platform per day: like and comment **kindly and genuinely** on other people's posts.
- **Reddit:** helpful comments only. Aim for ~**100 comment karma** by end of week.
- **Discord:** chat and help in 2–3 servers until people start to recognise you.
- **Facebook:** join groups, comment helpfully.

➡️ **You start promoting (the 10%) in Week 3.**

## 6. The content engine — posts WITHOUT video

Everything you post is one of **5 types**. No filming. Images come from **screenshots of the app** (value list, a WFL result, today's movers).

| # | Post type | What it is | How often | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HELP** | Just answer. No selling. | **~50%** | "That's a Win for you — same demand, and yours is rarer." |
| 2 | **VALUE / NEWS** | Share values + what went up/down. A screenshot of the app is perfect here. | **~25%** | "📈 Top 5 pets that went UP this week" + screenshot. |
| 3 | **DEBATE** | A real, honest opinion that makes people reply. | **~15%** | "Hot take: Frost Dragon is overrated 👇 change my mind." |
| 4 | **PROMO (soft)** | Mention the app — **only when it genuinely helps.** | **~10% max** | "I check trades free on AdoptMe Values — link in bio." |
| 5 | **IMAGE POST (optional, for Reddit/TikTok Photo Mode)** | A clean screenshot + short caption. | when you have one | "Is this a WIN or LOSS? 👀" + trade screenshot. |

**Easy weekly mix → per 10 posts: 5 help + 2 value + 2 debate + 1 promo** (and the 1 promo only if a natural moment comes up — otherwise skip it).

**⚠️ Rule for Debate posts:** it must be your **real, honest** opinion. **Never invent fake drama or fake claims to make people angry.** A friendly debate gets comments and is allowed. Fake drama gets you banned and damages the brand.

**Where your images come from (this replaces video):**
- Open the app → screenshot the **value list**, a **WFL result**, or **today's movers**.
- Crop it clean. Add a short caption or arrow/text if you want (free tools: your phone's editor, or Canva free).
- That screenshot is your "content." That's the whole engine — no editing skills needed.

## 7. Daily routine + scripts

### Daily routine (Week 3 onward) — about 3–4 focused hours

**Morning (~45 min)**
- Reply to any overnight comments/messages first (fast replies = more trust + reach).
- Post the day's **"value movers"** in our own server + any servers that allow it.

**Midday (~90 min)**
- **Discord:** **8–15 high-quality** helpful answers across the servers. (Soft-mention the app only where it genuinely helps *and* the server allows it.)
- **Reddit:** **10–15** helpful comments.

> **Why not more?** High reply counts from a new-ish account look like spam and get you banned. 10 genuinely helpful answers beat 40 lazy ones — every time.

**Afternoon (~60 min)**
- **Facebook:** 1 value/image post + ~10 helpful comments across rotating groups.
- (Optional) 1 TikTok Photo Mode image post.
- Reply again to any new comments.
- (Week 4+) Message 2 small creators (section 9) with the honest outreach offer.

> **Quality beats quantity.** 15 genuinely helpful answers are worth more than 40 lazy ones. If you're tired, do less but do it well.

### Scripts & templates (always reword — never paste the same line twice)

**Helpful replies (the 90%):**
- **WFL answer:** "That's a Win for you imo — [pet] has higher demand right now. (Double-checked on AdoptMe Values and it agreed 👍)"
- **Newbie help:** "Welcome! Tip: check *demand*, not just rarity. Lots of 'rare' pets are actually low-demand."
- **Debate reply:** "I disagree — [pet] is holding value because of the event coming up. What's everyone else seeing?"

**"Is it legit / what app do you use?" (be honest):**
- "I use AdoptMe Values — it's free and the WFL is usually spot on. Just double-check big trades."
- **If asked if you're on the team:** "Yeah, I help with it 🙂 happy to answer anything about how the values are made."

**Soft promo (the 10%):**
- "I check every trade free on AdoptMe Values — link in my bio."
- **Value post:** "📈 Today's movers: [pet] up · [pet] steady · [pet] down. Full list + free alerts in the app."

**Creator outreach DM (small creators, week 4+):**
- **First message:** "Hi [name]! Love your Adopt Me trading posts 🙌 I help with a free app, AdoptMe Values (WFL + live values + price alerts). Want free premium + a shoutout in our Discord if you mention it once? No pressure — happy to send details."
- Be honest it's a partnership. Offer free perks (premium, badge, shoutout). **Never pay cash to kids. Never pay for reviews.**

## 8. Links & tracking (so we know what works)

- Ask the manager for **one tracked link per platform** (a free shortener like Bitly/Dub gives click counts). Example: a Discord link, a Reddit link, a Facebook link.
- **Where links go:**
  - **Discord:** only in servers that allow it (full link, not a shortener if the server bans those).
  - **Reddit:** put the link **in a comment, not the post**; use the **full link** (Reddit auto-removes short links). Max **2–3 posts per week**.
  - **Facebook:** in the post is fine, where the group allows it.
  - **TikTok:** link goes in your **bio** only (never in the caption — TikTok hides those).
- **Check results weekly:** click counts from the shortener + install numbers from the store consoles (ask the manager for "view reports only" access).
