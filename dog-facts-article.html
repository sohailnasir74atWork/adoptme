FACT #3

💤 WAIT... SERIOUSLY?! Dogs Actually Dream!

You know when your dog twitches, whimpers, or moves their paws while sleeping? They're not just fidgeting — they're actually DREAMING! 🌙

Scientists at MIT studied dog brains and discovered that dogs go through the same sleep stages as humans — including the REM stage where dreams happen!

And get this... 🐿️ Researchers believe dogs dream about everyday activities — like chasing squirrels, playing fetch, and hanging out with YOU! Puppies and senior dogs dream even MORE than adult dogs. Sweet dreams, pupper! ✨
